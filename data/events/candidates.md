## Sourcing rule

**Primary sources only.** The gov.uk **content** API is authoritative for first publication; the **search** API's `public_timestamp` is the last-updated stamp and must never be used as an announcement date. Across this sweep the two differ by a median of 56 days and a maximum of 6,308 days.

News coverage is admissible for exactly one purpose: establishing that an announcement was trailed beforehand, which sets `leak_note` and feeds `anticipation_risk`. It is never the source for `announcement_timestamp_utc`.

1. **Step 2 leak check** — search the preceding 10 calendar days for reporting of the *substance*. If it appeared earlier, the event date is the earlier one and both timestamps are recorded. If disclosure was gradual, drop the event and say why.

3. **Step 5 fields** — `direction` needs a real mix of `tighten` and `loosen`, and each side needs enough events to reach significance alone. Three tightenings and one loosening yields `UNDERPOWERED` and tells you nothing.
